Organic Chemistry and Alkanes — 100 Questions

Questions 1–50: Multiple choice. Questions 51–75: True/False. Questions 76–100: Formula MCQs.

1. What is organic chemistry mainly the study of?
A. Metals and their compounds
B. Carbon and its compounds
C. Acids and bases only
D. Ionic compounds only
Answer: B. Carbon and its compounds
Explanation: Organic chemistry is the study of carbon and its compounds.

2. Which elements are found in a hydrocarbon?
A. Carbon and oxygen
B. Hydrogen and oxygen
C. Carbon and hydrogen
D. Carbon, hydrogen, and oxygen
Answer: C. Carbon and hydrogen
Explanation: A hydrocarbon contains only carbon and hydrogen.

3. Which statement is correct?
A. All organic compounds are hydrocarbons.
B. All hydrocarbons are organic compounds.
C. Hydrocarbons contain oxygen.
D. Hydrocarbons contain nitrogen.
Answer: B. All hydrocarbons are organic compounds.
Explanation: Hydrocarbons are organic compounds, but not every organic compound is a hydrocarbon.

4. Which substance is a hydrocarbon?
A. C2H4
B. C2H5OH
C. CH3COOH
D. CH3Cl
Answer: A. C2H4
Explanation: C2H4 contains only carbon and hydrogen.

5. Which substance is NOT a hydrocarbon?
A. CH4
B. C3H8
C. C2H4
D. C2H5OH
Answer: D. C2H5OH
Explanation: Ethanol contains oxygen, so it is organic but not a hydrocarbon.

6. Why is carbon able to form many compounds?
A. It has 1 valence electron.
B. It has 2 valence electrons.
C. It has 4 valence electrons.
D. It has 8 valence electrons.
Answer: C. It has 4 valence electrons.
Explanation: Carbon has four valence electrons and can form four bonds.

7. How many bonds can carbon normally form to achieve a stable octet?

8. Which is a property of organic compounds?

9. Why do many organic compounds react slowly?

22. What is a homologous series?

B. A sequence of organic compounds with similar characteristics
